# Formula And Model-Driven Specialization

This skill shares the common full workflow from:

- `../patent-method-runtime-boundary/references/workflow.md`
- `../patent-method-runtime-boundary/references/routing-map.md`
- `../patent-method-runtime-boundary/references/output-contracts.md`
- `../patent-method-runtime-boundary/references/checkpoints.md`

Use this specialization when the case is dominated by formulas, variables, models, simulation outputs, or equation-sensitive DOCX handling.

## Priority stages

- claim layout and claims drafting
- invention content
- embodiments and examples
- figures, legends, and result curves
- formulas and DOCX cleanup

## Local emphasis

- preserve formula completeness and formula display form together
- keep variable names, derivation direction, and figure labels synchronized
- explain model or simulation outputs in patent-suitable language
- do not let formula-heavy embodiment drafting drift away from the adopted claims

## Invention-content rule

When drafting `发明内容`, always follow:

- `../patent-method-runtime-boundary/references/stages/10-invention-content-drafting.md`
- `../patent-stage-invention-content/SKILL.md`

Do not use a separate local invention-content style.
