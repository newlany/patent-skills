# Process-Route Specialization

This skill shares the common full workflow from:

- `../patent-method-runtime-boundary/references/workflow.md`
- `../patent-method-runtime-boundary/references/routing-map.md`
- `../patent-method-runtime-boundary/references/output-contracts.md`
- `../patent-method-runtime-boundary/references/checkpoints.md`

Use this specialization when the case is dominated by process route rather than formulas or method-boundary ambiguity.

## Priority stages

- claim layout and claims drafting
- process detail
- embodiments and examples
- testing design
- results analysis

## Local emphasis

- keep the process chain executable in real manufacturing or treatment conditions
- make examples and comparative examples do real inventive-step work
- keep test items tightly linked to the claimed process distinctions
- use result tables to support the actual retained technical effects
- for material and formulation cases, add raw-material name, commercial grade or model, and supplier before `实施例` and `对比例` when available
- prefer domestic Chinese commercial products when they technically fit, and complete supplier or grade/model information only from real web evidence rather than inventing it
- commercial raw-material grades may be added to examples, but the grade must be tied to the claimed functional property and accompanied by generic fallback wording
- cite only real, usable test standards whose scope matches the tested metric, and put retained test data in tables
- for colorants, dispersions, fillers, catalysts, stabilizers, and similar additives, define active-component or moisture-control assumptions before comparing examples and comparative examples
