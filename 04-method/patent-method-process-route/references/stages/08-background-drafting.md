# Stage 8: Background Drafting

## Enter this stage when

- the user asks for stage 8
- the user asks for 背景技术
- the technical route and claim baseline are fixed

## Required baseline

- confirmed final disclosure
- confirmed claim set or claim baseline

## Task focus

- describe existing technical routes objectively
- describe concrete defects or limitations of prior routes
- align the stated problem with the problem later solved by the invention

## Deliverable

- background section of the specification

## Guardrails

- do not pre-state the invention's solution
- do not write marketing language

## Stop rule

Stop after the background section and wait for confirmation before invention content.
