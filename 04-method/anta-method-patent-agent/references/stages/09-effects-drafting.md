# Stage 9: Technical-Effects Drafting

## Enter this stage when

- the user asks for stage 9
- the user asks for 技术效果 or beneficial effects
- the claims and main technical route are fixed

## Required baseline

- confirmed claim set
- confirmed final disclosure
- confirmed inventive-step main line

## Task focus

- explain how the technical means produce the effects
- distinguish the effects of the main solution and narrower dependent solutions
- show why routine routes do not achieve the same result

## Deliverable

- technical-effects section for the invention content

## Guardrails

- do not cite numeric test data in this stage
- do not use direct legal labels such as inventive or non-obvious

## Stop rule

Stop after the effects section and wait for confirmation before process detail drafting.
