# Stage 6: Claim Layout

## Enter this stage when

- the user asks for stage 6
- the final disclosure is fixed
- the case needs a claim theme and ladder before formal drafting

## Required baseline

- confirmed final disclosure
- confirmed inventive-step main line

## Task focus

- determine the patent theme
- design the independent-claim skeleton
- decide which inventive features must enter claim 1
- design the dependent-claim ladder and fallback positions

## Deliverable

- claim-layout memo with theme, claim-count recommendation, claim-1 skeleton, dependent ladder, and claim-1 self-check

## Guardrails

- do not draft formal claims in this stage
- do not leave claim-1 creativity for the next stage

## Stop rule

Stop after the layout memo and wait for confirmation before formal claims.
