# Stage 6: Final Disclosure

## Enter this stage when

- the user asks for stage 6
- the main technical route is fixed
- the case needs one internally consistent technical baseline for claims and specification drafting

## Required baseline

- confirmed disclosure reconstruction
- confirmed method-boundary alignment
- confirmed core-feature analysis
- confirmed Search Checkpoint B recheck
- confirmed inventive-step analysis

## Task focus

- rewrite the disclosure into one internally consistent final version
- keep the main route natural and technically complete
- remove unsupported side branches
- preserve enough detail for claims, sections, examples, figures, and tests

## Deliverable

- final disclosure ready to support claims and specification drafting

## Guardrails

- do not turn the disclosure into a legal brief
- do not freeze the final disclosure while checkpoint B or the inventive-step main line is materially unresolved
- do not keep contradictory branches just for breadth

## Stop rule

Stop after the final disclosure and wait for confirmation before claim layout.
