# Stage 2: Disclosure Reconstruction

## Enter this stage when

- the user asks for stage 2
- stage 1 has identified gaps that must be repaired
- the case needs a technically runnable reconstructed disclosure

## Required baseline

- confirmed stage-1 disclosure analysis or an equivalent issue list
- original disclosure materials
- any user instruction on narrowing direction or preferred route
- confirmed Search Checkpoint A when the case is being prepared for inventive-step judgment or claim drafting

## Task focus

- repair the identified problems one by one
- supplement missing devices, data objects, parameters, control conditions, and transition outputs
- distinguish repaired facts from reasonable technical completion
- use the checkpoint-A search results to converge scattered routes into the strongest main route for later claim work
- state which branches are being retained, narrowed, or abandoned because of current-art pressure

## Deliverable

- supplementation memo
- complete reconstructed disclosure
- art-aware reconstruction rationale when checkpoint A was used

## Guardrails

- do not jump ahead to formal claims or section drafting
- keep inferred content separate from confirmed facts

## Stop rule

Stop after the reconstructed disclosure and wait for confirmation before method-boundary alignment.
