# Stage 14: Testing Design

## Enter this stage when

- the user asks for stage 14
- the user asks for 测试设计, 测试标准, or result-table design
- the examples and inventive main line are fixed

## Required baseline

- confirmed examples and comparative examples
- confirmed claim set
- confirmed inventive-effect logic

## Task focus

- choose only test items that support the claimed distinctions and effect logic
- use only real, usable test standards whose number and scope match the tested metric; do not invent standard numbers or force a standard that does not cover the property
- prefer suitable formal standards where appropriate; for Chinese patent drafts, use current GB or GB/T standards when available for the test item
- verify standard numbers and names from reliable public sources, standard platforms, official notices, standards databases, manufacturer/application notes, or other credible references before citing them
- write standard number, conditions, sample size, environment, equipment, and calculation method when available
- organize all test data in tables for later analysis
- design result-table columns around measured metrics. Do not keep process-label or argument-label columns unless the user asks for them or they are needed to identify samples
- when comparing commercial colorants, fillers, additives, or dispersions with different active concentrations, specify the normalization basis before the table, such as equal effective pigment content, equal active ingredient content, or equal total addition amount
- keep units and symbols consistent across the test method and table, such as `kg/m³` for apparent density where appropriate
- for preparation-heavy or material-preparation cases, keep the testing part limited to `测试项目和方法 -> 测试数据表`
- if disclosure data are missing, drafted example data may be completed from retained technical logic and field knowledge, but that status must be disclosed in the response
- when no measured data are provided, label drafted values as draft or to-be-confirmed in the response and avoid writing them as measured facts in the application

## Deliverable

- testing-standards section
- result-table design or draft results requested by the user, with data presented in table form

## Guardrails

- do not add irrelevant tests just to make the section look rich
- keep designed draft data distinct from real measured data
- do not cite a standard if its scope has not been checked against the tested material, sample form, and metric
- after deleting a metric or table column, scan the nearby test-method and results-analysis text for stale references to the deleted wording

## Stop rule

Stop after the testing design and wait for confirmation before results analysis.
