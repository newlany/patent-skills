# Response Handoff Guidance

## OA Response

Use `oa-response` when the task concerns an office action, D1/D2/D3, novelty, inventive step, claim amendment, or response brief.

The material digest must include:

- the office-action rejection points;
- the current claims;
- cited-reference facts with page or paragraph references when available;
- any proposed amendment basis;
- the desired response posture, such as amend, argue without amendment, or prepare alternatives.

Ask ChatGPT Pro to output:

- rejection-point decomposition;
- feature comparison table if useful;
- amendment options with basis and risk;
- main argument line;
- draft response paragraphs;
- residual weaknesses and facts that need checking.

## Correction

For correction or replacement-page tasks, keep the handoff narrow. ChatGPT Pro may help rewrite wording, but Codex should handle page scope, formatting, numbering, and DOCX replacement.

## Invalidity

Use `invalidity` for invalidation request, invalidity defense, oral-hearing preparation, requester arguments, or panel questions.

The material digest must include:

- target patent claim set;
- evidence list;
- asserted grounds;
- procedural posture;
- claim construction issues;
- facts that are confirmed and facts that remain uncertain.

Ask ChatGPT Pro to output:

- attack or defense theory;
- claim-feature mapping;
- likely counterarguments;
- oral-hearing short answers;
- evidence gaps.

## Response Guardrails

Tell ChatGPT Pro not to fabricate legal citations, document paragraph numbers, office-action facts, or evidence content. If a paragraph, page, figure, or claim basis is missing, it must mark the point as needing verification.
